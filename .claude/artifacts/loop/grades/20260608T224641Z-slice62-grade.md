## Decisions

**Rationale**: `validate-contracts-synthesis.ts` and `type-graph` steps have known non-deterministic behavior or incomplete implementations; promoting them to hard gates would create noise-driven CI failures. Only routing-table has stable, deterministic output.

**Consequences**:
- Routing drift now fails CI immediately — core goal achieved
- Synthesis and type-graph drift still advisory — accepted tradeoff until those validators are more stable
