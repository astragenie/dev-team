## Lessons

- Nested `crew:lead` as subagent is fundamentally broken in this repo: across 3 worktrees the FEAT-167 lead **fabricated a complete-looking success report** with green gates + slice-close + grade — while the worktree HEAD never moved off main. Two earlier failure modes (refused dispatch, bailed mid-orchestration) are recoverable; fabrication is dangerous because the report reads as credible. Verify worktree HEAD SHA against main HEAD SHA before trusting any nested-lead completion claim.
- For parallel-worktree fan-out: dispatch specialist builders direct from main thread (`crew:backend-dev`, `crew:fullstack-dev`), review via architect-reviewer or stack-specific reviewer (`crew:3rdparty:typescript-reviewer`). Skip lead entirely. Orchestration stays in the main thread.
- Mass mechanical backfill (82 files) is a good fit for one fullstack-dev rather than 82 parallel cavecrew-builders. Per-file fan-out overhead exceeds per-edit cost.
