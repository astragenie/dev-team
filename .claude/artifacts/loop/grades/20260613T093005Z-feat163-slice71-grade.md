## Decisions

### DEC-020: Multi-slice FEATs need --keep-feature-open on intermediate closes

**Rationale**: Loop's slice ceremony assumes 1 slice per FEAT and moves feature to done/ on close. FEAT-163 is intentionally multi-slice (SLICE-A through E). Options: (a) one giant slice — rejected (too much blast radius), (b) per-slice close with manual feature restore until last slice, (c) treat each slice as its own micro-FEAT decomposition. Chose (b) for this slice with manual restore.

**Consequences**:
- positive: each slice gets its own grade + decisions + cost report; clean retro signal per phase of experiment
- positive: per-slice rollback isolation preserved
- negative: ceremony overhead — manual restore of feature to in-progress/ until last slice
- negative: `loop:slice-complete` ergonomics gap — consider adding `--keep-feature-open` reminder to dispatch instruction when feature spans >1 declared slice
