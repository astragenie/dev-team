## Lessons

- **Ceremony e2e is expensive to build blind.** First builder burned 243k tokens exploring how `slice complete` satisfies its review/validation gates and returned ZERO code. Fix: pre-specify the gate recipe — the review gate is verdict-aware and satisfied by writing a `crew/reviews/<slice>.md` artifact with frontmatter `decision: approved`; no badge CLI, no agent needed. Capture recipes for hard-to-drive CLIs BEFORE dispatching a builder at them.
- **Review earns its keep on test-guards.** Reviewer caught two coverage-integrity holes that would have shipped a green-but-inert guard: (1) scenario silently skips in CI (dev-team CI has no `../runner-plugin` sibling); (2) AC-3 was a vacuous dir-non-empty check. A test that silently doesn't test is worse than no test.
- **"declared but never read" fired twice as a STALE mid-edit snapshot** (scenarioSliceCeremony, normalizeForScopeMatch) — both were actually wired (main() line 779; assert line 707). Verify via `tsc --noEmit` before trusting a transient diagnostic.
