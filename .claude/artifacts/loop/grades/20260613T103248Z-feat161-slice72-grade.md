## Decisions

### DEC-021: Parent-side stub recovery is part of the contract, not an exception

**Rationale**: Builder paused after work complete on SLICE-72. Parent updated the stub to `--decision pass --confidence high` via `crew write-handoff --update` and slice closed normally. This recovery is the EXPECTED flow per FEAT-161 risk #1 mitigation — pauses leave stubs, parent recovers. The recovery should be documented as a first-class flow, not treated as exceptional.

**Consequences**:
- positive: stub-on-entry pattern validated end-to-end on its own slice (real recurrence captured + recovered)
- positive: lead/orchestrator gains a deterministic recovery primitive instead of re-dispatch (which costs 150k tokens)
- negative: parent must know HOW to detect a stub vs missing artifact — depends on filename conventions + frontmatter `status: in-progress` check
- followup: document the recovery flow in lead.md or a dedicated SOP
